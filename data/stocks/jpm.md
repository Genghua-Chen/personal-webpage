# JPMorgan Chase & Co.

## Overview
JPMorgan Chase & Co. is the largest bank in the United States and one of the leading financial services firms globally. The company operates through Consumer & Community Banking, Corporate & Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management segments.

## Key Highlights
- Largest U.S. bank by assets and market capitalization
- Diversified revenue streams across banking, trading, and wealth management
- Market-leading investment banking and capital markets business
- Strong credit card franchise with Chase brand
- Industry-leading technology investments and digital capabilities

## Recent Performance
JPMorgan continues to benefit from higher interest rates, which have expanded net interest margins and profitability. The company has gained market share in investment banking and maintained its leadership position despite market volatility. Credit quality remains strong with loan loss provisions at normalized levels. The acquisition of First Republic Bank added significant West Coast presence.
