# Microsoft Corporation

## Overview
Microsoft Corporation develops, licenses, and supports software, services, devices, and solutions worldwide. The company operates through Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing segments.

## Key Highlights
- Leading cloud infrastructure provider with Azure platform
- Dominant position in enterprise software (Office 365, Teams, Dynamics)
- Strong AI capabilities through OpenAI partnership and Copilot integration
- Gaming division strengthened by Activision Blizzard acquisition
- LinkedIn professional network and GitHub developer platform

## Recent Performance
Microsoft continues to demonstrate strong growth driven by Azure cloud services and AI initiatives. The company's strategic partnership with OpenAI has positioned it as a leader in generative AI technology, with Copilot features being integrated across the product portfolio. Azure remains the second-largest cloud platform globally with consistent market share gains.
