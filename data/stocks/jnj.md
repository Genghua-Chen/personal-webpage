# Johnson & Johnson

## Overview
Johnson & Johnson is a global healthcare company operating in pharmaceutical, medical devices, and consumer health segments. The company develops, manufactures, and sells a broad range of products in the healthcare field worldwide.

## Key Highlights
- Diversified healthcare portfolio across pharmaceuticals and medical devices
- Strong pharmaceutical pipeline with multiple blockbuster drugs
- Industry-leading medical devices business
- 61 consecutive years of dividend increases (Dividend King)
- Recently spun off consumer health business (Kenvue) to focus on higher-margin segments

## Recent Performance
Johnson & Johnson has completed its strategic transformation by spinning off its consumer health business, allowing management to focus on higher-growth pharmaceutical and medical technology segments. The pharmaceutical pipeline remains robust with several high-potential drugs in late-stage development. Medical devices division shows steady growth despite elective procedure headwinds.
