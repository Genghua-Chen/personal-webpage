# Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company

## Overview
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) is the world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry. The company manufactures chips for leading technology companies globally, operating advanced fabrication facilities primarily in Taiwan.

## Key Highlights
- World's largest pure-play semiconductor foundry with ~60% market share
- Leading-edge process technology (3nm, 2nm in development)
- Key supplier to Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, and other major tech companies
- Expanding manufacturing capacity in the US, Japan, and Europe
- Critical role in global semiconductor supply chain

## Recent Performance
TSMC continues to benefit from strong demand for advanced chips, particularly for AI applications and high-performance computing. The company is ramping up 3nm production and seeing strong customer adoption. Geographic diversification efforts are progressing with new fabs under construction in Arizona, though the majority of production remains in Taiwan.

## Risk Factors
- Geopolitical tensions between Taiwan and China create existential risk
- Customer concentration (Apple represents ~25% of revenue)
- Massive capital expenditure requirements ($30B+ annually)
- Competition from Samsung and Intel's foundry ambitions
- Technology leadership challenge as Moore's Law slows
- Potential export restrictions and trade tensions
