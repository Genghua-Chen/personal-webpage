# Support & Resistance

## What Are They?

**Support** is a price level where buying pressure is strong enough to prevent the price from falling further. **Resistance** is a price level where selling pressure prevents the price from rising further.

```
Support    = price floor  (buyers step in here)
Resistance = price ceiling (sellers step in here)
```

## Why Do They Form?

- **Previous highs/lows:** Traders remember key price levels where reversals occurred.
- **Round numbers:** Psychological levels like $100, $50, $500 attract orders.
- **Moving averages:** The 50-day and 200-day MAs act as dynamic support/resistance.
- **High-volume nodes:** Areas where large amounts of stock changed hands.
- **Gap fills:** Price gaps often get "filled" later, acting as a magnet.

## Role Reversal

One of the most important concepts: when a support level is broken, it often becomes resistance — and vice versa.

> [!INFO]
> **Example:** Stock bounces off $50 three times (support). Eventually breaks below $50. Now when the stock rallies back to $50, sellers appear — the old support becomes resistance. This happens because trapped longs exit at break-even.

## How to Identify Levels

1. Look for price areas where the stock has reversed **multiple times** (more touches = stronger level).
2. Use higher timeframes (daily, weekly) for the most significant levels.
3. Zones are more reliable than exact price points — mark them as ranges, not single lines.
4. Consider the volume at the level — high-volume reversals create stronger levels.

## Trading Support and Resistance

### Bounce Trades

Enter near support expecting a bounce upward (or near resistance expecting a reversal down). Stop goes just beyond the level. Target is the next resistance/support.

### Breakout Trades

Enter when price breaks through a key level with conviction (volume confirmation). Stop goes just back inside the range. The old resistance becomes your first support target.

> [!WARNING]
> **Fakeouts:** Price often briefly breaks through a level before reversing. Wait for a candle close beyond the level, or a retest, before entering on breakouts.

## Quick Summary

- Support = floor, Resistance = ceiling.
- More touches = stronger level.
- Broken support → becomes resistance (role reversal).
- Use zones, not exact lines.
- Confirm breakouts with volume.
