# Candlestick Patterns

## Reading a Candlestick

```
High ─── wick (shadow)
     │
Open ┤  body (green = bullish, close above open)
     │  body (red   = bearish, close below open)
Close┘
     │
Low  ─── wick (shadow)
```

The body shows the open-to-close range. The wicks show the high and low extremes for the period.

## Single Candle Patterns

### Doji

Open ≈ Close; very small or no body. Wicks on both sides. Signals indecision — neither bulls nor bears won the period.

> [!INFO]
> A Doji is more meaningful at extremes (top of an uptrend = potential reversal) than in the middle of a range.

### Hammer / Hanging Man

Small body at the top, long lower wick (at least 2× body), little or no upper wick.

- **Hammer** (at bottom of downtrend) → bullish reversal signal. Buyers rejected lower prices.
- **Hanging Man** (at top of uptrend) → bearish warning. Same shape, opposite context.

### Shooting Star / Inverted Hammer

Small body at the bottom, long upper wick.

- **Shooting Star** (at top of uptrend) → bearish reversal. Buyers pushed price up but sellers took control.
- **Inverted Hammer** (at bottom of downtrend) → bullish potential.

### Marubozu

Large body with no wicks (or very small). Shows strong conviction: bullish Marubozu = buyers dominated the entire session.

## Two-Candle Patterns

### Engulfing Pattern

- **Bullish Engulfing:** A large green candle completely engulfs the prior red candle's body. Strong reversal signal at support.
- **Bearish Engulfing:** A large red candle engulfs the prior green candle. Reversal signal at resistance.

### Harami

Small candle inside the previous candle's body (opposite of engulfing). Signals momentum slowdown. Weaker than engulfing.

## Three-Candle Patterns

### Morning Star / Evening Star

- **Morning Star:** Large red → small candle (gap down) → large green. Bullish reversal.
- **Evening Star:** Large green → small candle (gap up) → large red. Bearish reversal.

### Three White Soldiers / Three Black Crows

- **Three White Soldiers:** Three consecutive strong bullish candles, each closing near its high. Strong bullish continuation.
- **Three Black Crows:** Three consecutive strong bearish candles. Strong bearish continuation.

## Key Rules for Using Candlesticks

- Context matters — a bullish pattern at resistance is not as reliable as one at support.
- Confirm with the next candle — wait for follow-through before entering.
- Volume confirmation strengthens any pattern.
- No pattern is 100% reliable — always use stops.

> [!WARNING]
> Candlestick patterns are probabilistic signals, not certainties. They are most powerful when they align with a key support/resistance level, a trend, and high volume.
