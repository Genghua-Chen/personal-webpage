# Market Hours & Sessions

## US Market Hours (ET)

```
Pre-Market:    4:00 AM – 9:30 AM ET
Regular Hours: 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET   ← main session
After-Hours:   4:00 PM – 8:00 PM ET
```

## Key Times Within the Day

### 9:30 – 10:30 AM — The Open

The most volatile and high-volume period. Overnight news, gaps, and pre-market moves get absorbed. Large institutional orders hit the market. Best for momentum trades but also very risky due to wide spreads and rapid reversals.

> [!WARNING]
> Many experienced traders avoid the first 15–30 minutes as the open can be chaotic and prone to fake moves. Let the market find direction first.

### 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM — Late Morning

Volatility tends to settle. The initial gap may be filled. Trend for the day often establishes itself. Better for breakout setups after the initial noise subsides.

### 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM — Lunch / Midday

Volume drops significantly. Price can chop in a range. Breakouts during this period are often less reliable (low volume = easy to fake). Best to reduce activity here.

### 2:00 – 3:00 PM — Afternoon

Volume picks back up. Fed announcements (if any) are often at 2 PM ET. Trend reversals often begin in this window.

### 3:00 – 4:00 PM — Power Hour

Volume surges again. Institutional rebalancing and MOC (Market on Close) orders drive significant activity. The day's trend can accelerate or sharply reverse.

## Pre-Market & After-Hours

- Lower volume → wider bid-ask spreads → easier to move price.
- Earnings and major news often release AH/PM — big gaps can occur.
- Options do **not** trade pre/post market (only regular hours).
- Moves in AH/PM do not always hold at the regular open — gaps can fill.

## Key Expiration Dates

```
0DTE  = options expiring today (same-day expiry)
Weekly = expire every Friday (Mon/Wed/Fri for SPX/NDX)
Monthly = expire 3rd Friday of each month
Quarterly OPEX: March, June, September, December
```

> [!INFO]
> Quarterly OPEX (options expiration) can cause unusual volatility and large end-of-day moves as institutions roll or close large positions.

## Quick Summary

- Main session: 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET.
- Most active: Open (9:30–10:30) and Power Hour (3:00–4:00).
- Lunch hour (12–2 PM): choppy, low-volume — reduce activity.
- Pre/AH: wider spreads, gaps possible, no options trading.
