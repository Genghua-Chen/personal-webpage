# The 3-5-7 Rule

## Overview

The **3-5-7 Rule** is a straightforward risk management framework that sets hard limits on how much capital you risk at three levels: per trade, across correlated positions, and as a maximum portfolio drawdown trigger.

## The Three Numbers

### 3% — Maximum Risk Per Trade

Never risk more than **3%** of your total account on any single trade. This ensures that even a string of consecutive losses cannot wipe out your account quickly.

```
Max Loss Per Trade = Account Size × 0.03
Example: $10,000 account → max risk = $300 per trade
```

### 5% — Maximum Correlated Exposure

Keep total exposure to **correlated** positions (same sector, same direction) under **5%** of the account. This prevents a single sector move from hitting multiple positions and blowing past your per-trade limits.

> [!INFO]
> **Example:** If you have three tech longs open simultaneously, their combined risk should not exceed 5% of your account, even if each individual position is within the 3% limit.

### 7% — Hard Stop on Total Account Drawdown

If your account drops by **7%** from any equity peak, *stop trading*. Step back, review your trades, identify what went wrong, and only return when you have a clear head. This circuit-breaker prevents emotional revenge trading from turning a bad week into a catastrophic loss.

## Why These Numbers?

- At 3% risk per trade, you need **34 consecutive losses** before losing your entire account — extremely unlikely with a reasonable strategy.
- The 7% drawdown stop gives you enough room for normal variance while catching genuine strategy failure early.
- Together they create a layered defense: trade level → sector level → portfolio level.

> [!TIP]
> Many professional traders use even tighter limits (1–2% per trade). The 3-5-7 rule is a good starting framework; tighten as your account grows.

## Quick Reference

| Number | What it limits |
|--------|----------------|
| **3%** | Max risk on a single trade |
| **5%** | Max correlated sector exposure |
| **7%** | Hard account drawdown circuit breaker |
