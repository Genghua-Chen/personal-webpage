# 1-2-3 Reversal Rule

## What is the 1-2-3 Rule?

The **1-2-3 reversal** is a price-action pattern used to identify potential trend reversals. It was popularized by Victor Sperandeo in his book *Trader Vic*. Three price swings signal that a trend is exhausted and ready to reverse.

## The Three Steps

### Step 1 — Break of the Trend Line

The existing trend line is broken. In a **downtrend** this means price breaks above the descending trendline; in an **uptrend** price falls below the rising trendline. This is the first warning the trend may be ending.

### Step 2 — Failure to Make a New Extreme

After breaking the trendline, price retraces and attempts to continue the old trend — but *fails* to make a new low (in a downtrend) or a new high (in an uptrend). This failure is the "2". It shows the trend is losing momentum.

### Step 3 — Break of the Prior Swing

Price reverses again and breaks through the swing high formed in Step 1 (for a downtrend reversal), or breaks below the swing low formed in Step 1 (for an uptrend reversal). This "3" confirms the reversal — it is the entry signal.

> [!TIP]
> **Entry:** at the break of Step 1's swing high/low (the "3" confirmation). **Stop:** just beyond the Step 2 extreme. **Target:** measure the height of the pattern and project from the breakout point.

## Downtrend Reversal Example

```
Prior downtrend
      ↘
       [1] Trendline break (price bounces up)
        ↘ [2] Lower high forms (failed rally, no new low)
          → [3] Price breaks above [1]'s high = BUY signal
```

## Uptrend Reversal Example

```
Prior uptrend
      ↗
       [1] Trendline break (price dips)
        ↗ [2] Higher low forms (failed dip, no new high)
          → [3] Price breaks below [1]'s low = SELL/SHORT signal
```

## Key Notes

- Works on **any timeframe** — the pattern is fractal.
- Volume confirmation: look for increasing volume on the Step 3 breakout.
- The pattern is stronger when Step 2 forms near a known support/resistance level.
- A clean 1-2-3 in the same direction as a higher-timeframe trend = higher probability.

> [!WARNING]
> **Common mistake:** Entering at Step 2 instead of waiting for Step 3 confirmation. Step 2 alone is not a reversal signal — price may still continue the old trend.

## Quick Summary

- **1** = Trendline break
- **2** = Failed attempt at new extreme
- **3** = Break of Step 1 swing → entry
